WHO WE ARE

About Our Organisation

Stanbic IBTC Pension Managers Limited is a Pension Fund Administrator in Nigeria and part of the Stanbic IBTC Group, a member of the Standard Bank Group.

We provide pension administration and fund management services, helping individuals and organisations prepare for retirement through appropriate pension solutions.

Our focus is to support contributors throughout their pension journey, from building retirement savings to accessing retirement benefits in accordance with applicable regulations.
