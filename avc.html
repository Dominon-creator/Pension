UNDERSTANDING AVC

What Are Additional Voluntary Contributions?

Additional Voluntary Contributions (AVCs) are extra pension contributions an eligible employee can make in addition to the regular mandatory contributions under the Contributory Pension Scheme. They provide an opportunity to increase retirement savings and work towards individual financial goals.
