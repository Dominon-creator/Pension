UNDERSTANDING THE PLAN

What Is a Dollar Pension Plan?

A dollar-denominated pension arrangement is designed for eligible customers who wish to make pension contributions in a foreign currency, subject to the product's applicable terms and regulatory requirements. It provides another way to approach long-term retirement planning for individuals whose income or financial commitments involve foreign currencies.
