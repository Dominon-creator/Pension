UNDERSTANDING PPP

What Is a Personal Pension Plan?

The Personal Pension Plan (PPP), also known as the Micro Pension Plan, is designed for eligible self-employed individuals and workers in the informal sector. It provides an opportunity to make pension contributions towards retirement, even without working for a formal employer.

IS THIS PLAN FOR YOU?

Who Can Participate?

The plan is designed for eligible individuals who earn income independently.

Business Owners

Individuals running their own small and medium-sized businesses.

Freelancers

Independent professionals earning income through freelance services.

Self-Employed Workers

Individuals who work independently and manage their own income.

Informal Sector Workers

Eligible workers in different informal occupations and trades.
