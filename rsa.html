UNDERSTANDING YOUR RSA

What is a Retirement Savings Account?

A Retirement Savings Account (RSA) is an individual pension account opened with a licensed Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) to receive pension contributions on behalf of an eligible contributor. The account is designed to help build retirement savings throughout your working life.

WHY IT MATTERS

Benefits of an RSA
