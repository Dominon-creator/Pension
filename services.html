Our Pension Solutions

At Stanbic IBTC Pension Managers, we provide pension solutions designed to help you save, invest and prepare for a financially secure future.

Retirement Savings Account (RSA)

A Retirement Savings Account is designed for employees in the public and private sectors under the Contributory Pension Scheme. Your employer and you make contributions towards building your retirement savings.

Learn More

Personal Pension Plan (PPP)

A flexible retirement savings solution for self-employed individuals, business owners, traders, artisans, freelancers and employees who want to make additional voluntary contributions towards retirement.

Learn More

Dollar Pension Plan

A pension savings solution designed for eligible Nigerians earning in foreign currency, including Nigerians in the diaspora. It provides an opportunity to save towards retirement in US dollars.

Additional Voluntary Contributions (AVCs)

Want to do more for your future? AVCs allow eligible RSA contributors to make additional contributions to boost their retirement savings beyond mandatory pension contributions.

INVESTING FOR YOUR FUTURE

Understanding the Multi-Fund Structure

Your pension savings are invested according to applicable pension fund rules, with fund options designed to consider factors such as your age, retirement status and investment risk profile.

RSA Fund I & II

Investment options for eligible active contributors, with different investment risk and return profiles.

RSA Fund III & IV

Fund III is for eligible active contributors aged 50 and above, while Fund IV is designed for retirees.

RSA Fund V

The Personal Pension Plan fund, with Conservative and Growth options for eligible contributors.

RSA Fund VI

Ethical, non-interest-bearing investment options available in Active and Retiree categories.

RSA Fund VII

A fund designed for eligible contributors earning in foreign currency, including Nigerians in the diaspora.
